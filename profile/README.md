## Hey, we're CodeFlanders

We're Ruben, Wissem, and Zeynal. We build websites, webshops, and software for businesses across Europe.

Some projects start with a new website. Others start with someone asking why they're still copying the same data into three different programs. We work on both.

```ts
const team = ["Ruben", "Wissem", "Zeynal"];
const inbox = "team@codeflanders.com";
const unnecessaryManualWork = false; // that's the goal
```

### Things we can help with

- **Websites and webshops.** Somewhere customers can understand your business, ask for a quote, or place an order.
- **Custom apps.** Work orders, hours, materials, photos. Tools that fit what happens on the job.
- **Automation and integrations.** Connect your programs and take recurring tasks off your team's hands.
- **Digital processes and dashboards.** Get information out of scattered emails, files, and exports and into something usable.
- **AI.** Use existing AI tools where they help, with clear limits and someone checking the results.

There are [interactive examples on our website](https://www.codeflanders.com/diensten#voorbeelden) if you'd rather try something than read about it.

### Working with us

You talk directly to us, and we're the ones who build it.

We start with a free online conversation. Then we look at the process with you and agree on the scope, budget, and timing before building. You see the work as it develops. If something changes, we discuss it first.

At the end, you get a clear handover. We agree on maintenance separately.

A small job is fine too. Connecting two programs or fixing one annoying step can be the whole project.
